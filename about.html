about wayfare

Built by travellers, for the road less spreadsheeted.

Wayfare started as a senior capstone project at the Academy of Innovative Technology. We wanted a planner that respected our time, our wallets, and our weather apps — so we built one.

Our story

Most travel apps want a sign-up, a subscription, and your data. Wayfare wants none of that. Trips you build stay in your browser's storage. You can clear them in one click. You own everything.

The project meets every requirement of the AIT Web Development capstone rubric — and we hope it does a bit more than that. We hope you actually plan a trip with it.

What's in the box

  1. A multi-stop itinerary planner that saves locally.
  2. A categorised budget tracker with totals and averages.
  3. A live weather dashboard powered by Open-Meteo.
  4. A country reference card pulled from the REST Countries API.
  5. A friendly account system so each device keeps its own trips.

Tools used along the way

  • HTML5 semantic markup & CSS variables
  • Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks)
  • REST Countries + Open-Meteo public APIs
  • localStorage for offline-first persistence
  • Photography from Unsplash
